# 🧱 The Workbench Philosophy

> *Architecture is the set of decisions which, if made wrong, cause your project to fail.*  
> — Eoin Woods  
>
> *Architects sketch.*  
> — Gregor Hohpe  

---

## 1. Architecture is decisions, not diagrams

Diagrams are the shadows of decisions.  
In Workbench, the **decision** is the atomic unit of architecture.  
C4 views, maps, and visualizations are generated *from* those decisions — never the other way around.  
By capturing rationale first, the architecture stays honest, explainable, and traceable.

---

## 2. Architects shape, teams decide

Workbench follows the **Shape Up cadence**.  
Architects and tech leads define **principles, boundaries, and appetite** — the abstract intent.  
Teams fill the space with **concrete decisions**: ADRs that realize those principles in code.  
This keeps ownership balanced and context alive.

---

## 3. Documentation lives with the work

Documentation isn’t a separate deliverable; it’s a living artifact.  
Every principle, decision, and outcome is linked, searchable, and evolves alongside the system.  
No stale wikis. No forgotten rationale.  
Just living knowledge that reflects how the product *really* works.

---

## ✳️ In short

**Workbench** is where architecture is shaped by decisions,  
guided by principles,  
and kept alive by the work itself.
